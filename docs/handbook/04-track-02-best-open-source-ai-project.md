# Track 02 | Best Open-Source AI Project

> 
> **BEST OPEN-SOURCE AI PROJECT**

> Choose one primary problem statement. Show meaningful use of open-source or open-weight AI in your solution.

### Problem statement options

<details open><summary>O1. Autonomous Agent Swarms</summary>

Integrate a collaborative multi-agent approach where independent agents communicate, coordinate and work autonomously toward a common goal. Demonstrate meaningful agent-to-agent interaction and collective task execution.

</details>

<details open><summary>O2. Adaptive AI with Memory and Self-Correction</summary>

Build an open-source AI solution that retains relevant context, learns from feedback or identifies and corrects mistakes. Show how AI adapts its responses or actions over time instead of treating every interaction independently.

</details>

<details open><summary>O3. Trustworthy and Verifiable AI</summary>

Integrate mechanisms that improve transparency, reliability or accountability of AI-generated outputs or decisions. Demonstrate how users can understand, verify or evaluate information produced by an AI system.

</details>

<details open><summary>O5. Open Innovation with Open-Source AI</summary>

Build an original AI-powered solution in any problem domain with meaningful use of open-source or open-weight AI technology.

</details>

### Submission requirements

Projects must be built between 13 and 18 October 2026. Submit a public GitHub repository, a working deployment URL and your selected problem statement when the submission portal is announced.
